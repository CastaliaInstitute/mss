# PhotoPainter event backgrounds · Winter 2026

Device backgrounds are stored as 800 × 480 six-color PNGs in
`public/mss-daily-art/events/`. Each entry in
`photopainter-event-backgrounds.json` records its MSS UID, dates, source image,
and device asset path. The original MSS and book artwork remains unchanged.

Most device files are resized/cropped versions of the event artwork already
referenced by `MSS.ics`; if no event image exists, the generator selects a
matching Winter 2026 book illustration. Two original interpretive backgrounds
were generated for missing imagery. They are not documentary depictions of a
real event or religious practice.

## Gita Jayanti

Source: `gita-jayanti-photopainter-background-v1.png` (generated with built-in
ImageGen, 2026-11-10). The image is an abstract seasonal scene of light and
reflection; it does not portray a deity, sacred text, or ritual.

Prompt:

> Wide 5:3 landscape background for an e-paper seasonal calendar event page, designed to crop cleanly to 800x480. Create an original, respectful, non-documentary illustration suggesting Gita Jayanti through quiet light, reflection, and winter color: deep indigo blue fading into warm saffron and muted marigold, delicate gouache and watercolor washes on fine paper, subtle golden light and restrained botanical forms near the left edge, softly textured distant landscape. Keep the right half calm, low-detail, and light enough to sit behind a separate opaque text panel. No people, no deity, no sacred text or lettering, no symbols, no buildings, no borders, no words. Editorial illustration, elegant, serene, high contrast suitable for conversion to a six-color e-paper display.

## Noaptea Lupilor

Source: `noaptea-lupilor-photopainter-background-v1.png` (generated with
built-in ImageGen, 2026-11-10). The image is an atmospheric winter forest with
a distant wolf; it does not depict a gathering, rite, or cultural practice.

Prompt:

> Wide 5:3 landscape background for an e-paper seasonal calendar event page, designed to crop cleanly to 800x480. Create an original, non-documentary winter-night illustration for the calendar entry titled Noaptea Lupilor (Night of Wolves): a moonlit forest clearing, quiet snow, a few natural wolf tracks leading toward a small distant wolf silhouette between evergreens, deep indigo and blue-gray with restrained silver moonlight and a touch of warm ochre in the sky. Refined gouache and watercolor on fine paper, elegant storybook editorial art, not scary. Keep the right half calm and low-detail for a separate opaque text panel. No people, no ritual, no costumes, no cultural symbols, no text, no lettering, no border, no depiction of a real gathering.
