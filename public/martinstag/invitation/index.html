Martinstag: Lanterns & a Wild Goose Chase

November 10–11 · Two days of light, kindness, and adventure.

Download invitation image · Read the host scripts

November 10 · Lantern Evening

  • Afternoon: Collect pine cones.
  • At dusk: Lantern walk.
  • Evening: Stories with Martin the Porch Goose, warm cider, pretzels, and roasted chestnuts.

November 11 · The Wild Goose Chase

Morning gifts of cloaks for the children. Martin has run away, afraid he will be eaten! Follow his clues attached to the pine cones, wearing your cloak and sounding your goose calls.
